About

Markets price
the unknowable.

Digital assets are the frontier of imperfect knowledge. Valuations rest on fragmented data, half-formed theses, and shifting consensus. Everyone is guessing — most in the same direction.

We do not predict. We position. Sized for asymmetry, built to survive being wrong, structured to compound when we are not.

The canon excludes what is inconvenient. So does the market. We invest where both have looked away.

I

Against the room

Crowded trades pay nothing. We take the other side of consensus and hold long enough to be proven right.

II

Asymmetric by design

Risk is not eliminated. It is chosen. Every position is built to lose little and earn much.

III

Time, not timing

We do not call tops. We do not call bottoms. We hold theses through cycles and let mispricing correct itself.

Approach

"The market is a device for transferring money from the impatient to the patient — and from the credulous to the skeptical."

Research

Bottom-up fundamental analysis of digital asset protocols, teams, and token economics. We read what others skip and ask questions others consider settled.

Risk

Asymmetric positioning with defined downside. We do not seek to eliminate risk — we seek to be paid for the risks we choose to take.

Horizon

Medium to long-term. We are not indifferent to price; we are indifferent to short-term noise. Conviction is built over months, not hours.
